## 🌟 About Us

DevForge-Labs is a collaborative development organization founded by passionate Computer Science students who believe in learning through building.

We design, develop, and maintain real-world software projects while continuously exploring modern technologies, software architecture, open-source practices, and product development.

Our goal is simple:

> Turn ideas into products and students into engineers.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

- Build meaningful software that solves real problems
- Learn industry-standard development workflows
- Practice collaborative software engineering
- Explore emerging technologies and frameworks
- Create a strong portfolio of impactful projects
- Foster a culture of innovation and continuous learning

## 🚧 Current Projects

### 📚 NotesSharing App

A social-first academic resource sharing platform where students can discover, upload, organize, and access quality educational notes and study materials.

#### Features

- User Authentication
- PDF Upload & Storage
- Personalized Feed
- Bookmarks & Favorites
- Search & Discovery
- Modern Social-Inspired UI
- Firebase Backend Integration
- Real-Time Data Synchronization
